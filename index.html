Open dataset

Since 2002, KoFlux towers have measured the carbon, water and energy that Korean ecosystems exchange with the air above them.

This portal shares 83 site-years of quality-controlled eddy covariance data from 21 forest and cropland sites, collected between 2011 and 2024.
